## Chapter 1 — Allah Loves the Clean
**Summary:** Cleanliness is part of our deen — being clean makes Allah happy and makes us feel wonderful. Sets a joyful, non-scolding tone: getting clean is a happy thing we get to do, not a chore.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her twirling happily in a sunlit, tidy room, arms open, gentle golden light around her, small sparkles in the air, a neatly made bed and tidy shelf in the background. Mood: pure joy and freshness. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 2 — Washing My Hands
**Summary:** When we wash (before eating, after playing, after the bathroom, when we come home) and how — soap, bubbles, between fingers, counting to twenty.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her at a child-height bathroom sink washing her hands with fluffy white soap bubbles, water droplets sparkling, standing on a small step stool, delighted expression. A soap bottle with a simple leaf symbol sits by the sink. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 3 — Wudu, My Special Wash
**Summary:** A gentle first introduction to wudu — the beautiful wash Muslims do before prayer — walking through the steps simply: hands, mouth, nose, face, arms, head, ears, feet.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her performing wudu at a low sink, cupping water in her small hands, water drops catching light, a peaceful reverent expression, a soft prayer rug visible in the background of a warm bathroom. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 4 — Bath Time Is the Best Time
**Summary:** Regular bathing, washing the whole body, gentle soap, and making bath time fun instead of a fight. Drying properly afterward.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her wrapped snugly in a large fluffy cream towel after a bath, hair slightly damp, laughing warmly, steam and a few soap bubbles in the air, a bathtub with a rubber duck in the soft-blue background. Fully modest — completely wrapped in the towel. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 5 — My Shining Teeth
**Summary:** Brushing morning and night, how to brush, and the sunnah of the miswak — the Prophet ﷺ loved clean teeth too.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her brushing her teeth at the mirror with a pink toothbrush, toothpaste foam, standing on her step stool, sparkle effects on her smile in the mirror reflection. A small natural miswak stick rests in a cup by the sink. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 6 — My Beautiful Hair
**Summary:** Washing hair, gentle brushing without tears, and braiding — hair care as self-love time, maybe with Mama's help.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her seated on a cushion while her mother (warm brown skin, kind face, soft pink hijab) gently braids her hair, both smiling, a wooden comb and hair ribbons on a small table beside them, cozy warm room. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 7 — Neat Nails, Soft Skin
**Summary:** Keeping nails short and clean (a sunnah!), washing hands and face, and a little lotion for soft skin.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her sitting at a small table while her mother trims her fingernails with a gentle child-safe clipper, the girl watching with curiosity, a small bottle of lotion with a leaf symbol nearby, soft afternoon light. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 8 — Clean Clothes Every Day
**Summary:** Fresh clothes daily, putting dirty clothes in the hamper (not the floor!), and folding — first steps of caring for what we wear.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her happily placing folded clean clothes into a low dresser drawer, a woven laundry hamper beside her, sunlight through a window, a few small neat stacks of colorful modest children's clothing. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 9 — Bathroom Manners
**Summary:** The adab of the bathroom, taught gently: privacy, entering and leaving politely, cleaning ourselves properly with water, and always washing hands after.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her outside a bathroom door in a warm hallway, hand on the door frame, looking back with a sweet polite smile, a small water pitcher visible on the clean bathroom floor inside, soft colors, completely modest and gentle. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 10 — My Clean Room
**Summary:** Making the bed, toys back in their basket, books on the shelf — a clean room feels peaceful, and the angels love clean places.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER]. Show her proudly smoothing the blanket on her small neatly-made bed, a basket of tidied toys at the foot of the bed, books lined on a low shelf, morning light, a satisfied proud smile. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"

## Chapter 11 — Sweet Sleep
**Summary:** The bedtime routine — bath or wash, teeth, pajamas, du'a before sleep — and why rest makes us strong and kind tomorrow.

**Prompt:** "[CHARACTER, but wearing soft lavender pajamas]. Show her tucked into bed with a cream blanket, hands together in gentle du'a, eyes softly closed, a crescent moon and stars visible through the window, a small lamp glowing warm, utterly peaceful. [STANDING INSTRUCTIONS]"
